# Product vs Service - Complete Guide for Shop Owners

## Overview

When adding items to your shop in Duwaz marketplace, you have two options:

1. **📦 Physical Products** - Items that need to be physically delivered (snacks, drinks, books, accessories, etc.)
2. **🔧 Services** - Services that are negotiated directly with customers (tutoring, consulting, repairs, design, etc.)

---

## Physical Products

### What Are Physical Products?

Physical products are tangible items that customers purchase and you deliver to them.

**Examples:**
- Snacks and drinks
- Books and stationery
- Clothing and accessories
- Electronics
- Food items
- Crafts and handmade goods

### Product Requirements

When adding a physical product, you must provide:
- ✅ **Product Name** - What you're selling
- ✅ **Price** - Cost in South African Rands (R)
- ✅ **At Least One Image** - How the product looks
- ✅ **Stock Quantity** - How many you have available
- ✅ **Category** - Category (Food, Books, Clothes, etc.)
- ✅ **Description** - Details about the product

### Product Process

1. Customer browses your products
2. Customer sees images and price
3. Customer adds product to cart
4. Customer selects delivery method:
   - 🏪 Collection - Pick up from your location
   - 💵 Cash on Delivery - Pay driver when delivered
   - 💳 Pay Online - Pay with card via Yoco

5. You receive order and prepare item
6. Item is delivered to customer
7. Customer receives and rating is confirmed

---

## Services

### What Are Services?

Services are offerings where you negotiate directly with customers. No stock, no delivery, just communication.

**Examples:**
- Tutoring (Math, English, Science, languages)
- Consulting (business, career, academic)
- Design work (graphics, UI/UX, logo design)
- Repair services (phone, computer, appliances)
- Fitness training (personal training, yoga classes)
- Photography (portfolio, event coverage)
- Writing services (essays, creative content)
- Tech support (coding help, IT assistance)
- Beauty services (hair, makeup, nails)
- Music lessons (guitar, piano, vocals)

### Service Requirements

When adding a service, you only need:
- ✅ **Service Name** - What service you offer
- ✅ **Price** - Your hourly rate or per-project rate (in R)
- ❌ **NO Images Required** - Services don't need photos
- ❌ **NO Stock** - Services aren't physical inventory
- ✅ **Category** - Category (Tutoring, Design, etc.)
- ✅ **Description** - Details about what you offer

### Service Process

1. Customer browses your services
2. Customer sees service name, description, and price
3. Customer clicks **"Message Provider"** or **"Contact Seller"**
4. Customer fills in an inquiry form with:
   - What they need help with
   - Their availability
   - Budget
   - Any special requirements
5. Message is sent to you
6. You respond with:
   - Availability
   - Pricing confirmation
   - Timeline/schedule
7. You negotiate terms directly
8. Once agreed, customer rates you
9. You get paid after service is delivered

## Step-by-Step: Adding a Product

### For a Physical Product (📦)

1. Go to your **Shop Dashboard**
2. Click **"Add Product"**
3. **Toggle to: 📦 Physical Product** (should be default)
4. Fill in:
   - **Name**: "Chocolate Chip Cookies"
   - **Price**: "45.00"
   - **Category**: "Snacks & Confectionery"
   - **Description**: "Freshly baked cookies with real chocolate chips"
   - **Stock**: "20"
5. **Upload Images**: Click to add up to 4 photos of your cookies
6. Click **"Add Product"**
7. ✅ Product is now live!

---

## Step-by-Step: Adding a Service

### For a Service (🔧)

1. Go to your **Shop Dashboard**
2. Click **"Add Product"**
3. **Toggle to: 🔧 Service** ← Important!
4. Fill in:
   - **Name**: "One-on-One Math Tutoring"
   - **Price**: "150.00" (per hour)
   - **Category**: "Services" or "Tutoring"
   - **Description**: "Grade 10-12 Maths tutoring. Specializing in Algebra and Calculus. Online and in-person options available."
5. ⏭️ **Skip Images** - Services don't need photos!
6. Click **"Add Service"**
7. ✅ Service is now live!
8. Wait for customers to message you

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I sell both products and services from the same shop?

**A:** Yes! Many shops do both. For example:
- A tutor who sells study materials (products) + offers tutoring (services)
- A designer who sells templates (products) + offers custom design (services)
- A fitness instructor who sells protein powder (products) + offers personal training (services)

### Q: What if I want to change a Product to a Service later?

**A:** You can edit any item by clicking the pencil icon next to it. You can toggle between Product and Service types. ✅

### Q: Do I need to specify delivery address for Services?

**A:** No! For services, customers message you directly to arrange the meeting location or online call. The system won't ask for delivery address.

### Q: Can services have stock?

**A:** Services are unlimited - you don't set a stock quantity. However, you might have limited availability (certain days/hours) which you communicate when replying to customer messages.

### Q: How long should a service description be?

**A:** **Keep it concise but informative:**
- ❌ Too short: "Tutoring"
- ✅ Good: "One-on-one tutoring for high school maths. Experienced with Grade 10-12 syllabus. Specializing in algebra and calculus."
- ❌ Too long: A paragraph essay

### Q: What if a customer doesn't message me about a service?

**A:** Your service just sits in the marketplace until someone inquires. Keep descriptions clear and prices fair to attract customers!

### Q: Can I offer the same thing as both Product and Service?

**A:** Sure! For example:
- **Product**: Pre-made study guides (physical PDF or printed)
- **Service**: Custom study guide creation (tailored to student's needs)

Or:
- **Product**: Photography prints (physical delivery)
- **Service**: Photo shoot booking (service delivery)

## Pro Tips

### For Physical Products:
1. **Use good lighting** - Take photos in natural light
2. **Show multiple angles** - Customers like seeing the product from different sides
3. **Include size reference** - A coin or hand in the photo shows scale
4. **Stock accurately** - Be honest about inventory
5. **Prices competitively** - Check similar products in the marketplace

### For Services:
1. **Be specific about what you offer** - "Tutoring" is vague; "Grade 11 Physics tutoring" is clear
2. **Include relevant experience** - "5 years teaching experience" builds trust
3. **Mention your availability** - "Available weekends" or "Online and in-person"
4. **Respond quickly** - Customer inquiries = potential sales! Aim for <2 hour response time
5. **Have a base price** - You can negotiate, but give customers a starting point

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Service can't be added - pictures won't load"

**Solution:**
- Services DON'T need pictures! If you're trying to add a service, just skip the image upload
- Leave the image fields empty
- Make sure you selected the 🔧 **Service** toggle

### Problem: "Product won't save"

**Solution:**
- ✅ Check that you uploaded at least ONE image
- ✅ Check that stock quantity is entered
- ✅ Check that product name and price are filled in
- ✅ Make sure you selected 📦 **Product** toggle
- If still failing, try refreshing the page

### Problem: "Customers aren't messaging me about my service"

**Solution:**
- Make your description more specific and detailed
- Ensure your price is competitive
- Make sure your service appears in the Services section
- Ask existing customers to leave reviews
